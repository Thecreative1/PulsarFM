Frequently asked questions

What is the best music to study to?

Lo-fi, chillhop, downtempo and classical: music without lyrics that doesn't compete with what you're reading. The eight stations on this page were picked for exactly that.

How can I listen to lo-fi radio online for free?

Open Pulsar FM in your browser, pick I Love Chillhop or SomaFM Groove Salad and press play. Free and distraction-free.

Can I study with Pulsar FM on my phone?

Yes, in any browser on your phone. Add Pulsar FM to your home screen and it opens like an app, no app store involved.
