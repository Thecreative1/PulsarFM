TSF Jazz is the reference jazz station in Paris, on air 24 hours a day. Pulsar FM also has Jazz Radio France, from swing to soul, and the more eclectic FIP Jazz.

How can I listen to jazz radio online for free?

Open Pulsar FM in your browser, pick a jazz station and press play. Live, free and no sign-up.

Can I listen to jazz radio on my phone?

Yes, in any browser, on desktop or phone. Tap the star on TSF Jazz and it stays in your favourites for next time.
