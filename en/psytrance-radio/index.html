Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between goa trance and psytrance?

Goa trance is the original 90s sound: melodic, eastern and hypnotic. Psytrance is its modern evolution, faster and heavier. Goa-Base plays the classic; Hirschmilch Psy plays today's sound.
