# PulsarFM

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

Listeners use PulsarFM for free online radio, a retro Webamp player and Milkdrop visuals. The editorial recommendations help Portuguese-speaking listeners choose equipment for music and entertainment. The radio remains the primary experience.

## Brand personality

Neon, retro, independent. Preserve the existing synthwave identity, Share Tech Mono typography and informal Portuguese voice documented in CLAUDE.md.

## Design principles

- Integrate recommendations through useful editorial content and a discreet footer entry.
- Keep the player, primary navigation and station controls intact.
- Avoid aggressive banners, shop layouts, invented offers and unsupported product claims.
- Store merchant and product data centrally; require editorial review before publishing feed products.
- Explain affiliate commissions beside commercial content and respect the existing analytics consent preference.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Retain readable contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible links and visible focus. New editorial pages must work on narrow screens and without JavaScript.
